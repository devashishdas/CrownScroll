**CrownScroll — Navigate Long ChatGPT Conversations with Ease**

Tired of scrolling through long ChatGPT conversations? CrownScroll adds a floating navigation tool that makes moving between messages faster, easier, and more precise.

Inspired by the mechanical crown of a classic watch, CrownScroll introduces a unique vertical thumbwheel that lets you navigate lengthy conversations using your mouse, keyboard, or a simple drag.

**Key Features**

- **Mechanical Crown Wheel:** Scroll using a compact, vertically mounted, knurled thumbwheel.
- **Instant Navigation:** Jump directly to the top or bottom of a conversation.
- **Page-by-Page Scrolling:** Move through long responses with dedicated controls.
- **Mouse and Keyboard Support:** Use the mouse wheel, drag gestures, or arrow keys when the wheel is focused.
- **Two Navigation Styles:** Switch between the Crown Wheel and Classic Navigation buttons.
- **Customizable Themes:** Choose light, dark, or system appearance.
- **Adjustable Sensitivity:** Customize scrolling speed to your preference.
- **Floating Interface:** Position or minimize the navigation toolbar.
- **Smart Scroll Detection:** Identify the conversation area and manually select it when necessary.
- **Toolbar Recovery:** Restore the floating controls if they disappear.



**Privacy First**

CrownScroll works locally in your browser. No account, subscription, or external cloud service is required. The extension does not include analytics or transmit conversation content to an external server.

**Who Is CrownScroll For?**

Researchers, students, developers, writers, and anyone who works with long ChatGPT conversations.

**Compatibility**

Designed for ChatGPT in Google Chrome and compatible Chromium browsers. It does not operate inside the native ChatGPT desktop application.

CrownScroll is an independent third-party extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.
